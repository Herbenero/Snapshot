How Snapshot Works

Flash Point

5 rounds, 3 darts each round. A unique random target from 1–20 is assigned to each round (no repeated targets). Select Single, Double, Triple, Quad, or Miss to score on the target. Highest total wins.

9 Dart Century

9 darts total across 3 turns. Get exactly 100 or as close as possible without going over. Going over 100 is a bust and the player is out. A dart outside the scoring area resets the player's score to zero. Bounce-outs count as miss with no points.

Quickdraw

3 rounds, 3 darts each round. Standard scoring with fast turn submission. Final standings show each player horizontally with scores for all three games in columns.
